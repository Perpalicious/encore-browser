2dRead on the trade-offs
3-up (2b) is the sweet spot
More than doubles what 2-up shows, the photo is still big enough to identify an object, and the title survives at 11px over two lines. Only the bucket chip is lost — it lives in the detail sheet anyway. Tap target is 113px square, far above the 44px floor.
4-up (2c) stops being a card list
At 84px the title can no longer carry the lot, so the photo does. Resale moves onto the image in a light gradient plate — a contact sheet you skim for shapes and prices, then tap. Condition survives as the coloured lid. Honest warning: 10px titles are near the limit for a phone at arm’s length in bad light, and long brand names truncate hard.
Recommendation
Ship Rows as the default, 3-up as the card default, and keep 2 and 4 as one-tap steps — the stepper is 3 characters wide, costs nothing, and lets you switch mid-auction. Rows still win on pure throughput because the title gets full width; cards win when you’re recognising objects.
